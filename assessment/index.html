FAQs

Common questions

How much of my time does this take?

A short, complimentary session. That is it on your end until we send your written summary a day or two later. If you choose the optional deep dive, expect several hour-long sessions so we can get into your data and systems in detail.

Who needs to be on the session?

You, and ideally your CFO. If you can, bring whoever knows your systems and reporting too, often someone in finance or operations. If it is just you, that is fine to start.

Do we have to change our systems to do this?

No. This is a read on what you already have, not a migration. Nothing about your systems has to change to get started.

Why can't we just ask AI to assess this ourselves?

You can, and you will get a competent, generic answer. What it cannot do is tell you which of your goals is worth doing first, because it has not seen your data or your business. We work inside manufacturing, distribution, and aerospace companies your size. We know what a typical ERP implementation leaves out, where the real numbers end up living, and which AI ideas have actually held up in production versus which ones demo well and stall six months in. That judgment comes from having been in a lot of these companies. An honest read on your situation takes someone who has enough other situations to compare it to.

Is our information safe?

Yes. We will sign a mutual NDA before you share anything, and we treat everything you share as confidential and use it only for your Blueprint. Nothing is shared outside infoFluency, and we do not use your data to train anything. In most cases we do not need copies of your data at all. The Blueprint is built from conversations about where your data lives and how it moves, not from extracts of it. If we do need to look at something directly, we agree on what and how first.

What do we walk away with?

A shortlist of where AI can help, an honest read on whether your data can support it, a map of your systems, a one-page blueprint, and one clearly scoped first project. Plain language, no jargon.

What if we are already in good shape?

We will tell you. The honest read is the point. You will still leave knowing where you stand and what, if anything, is worth doing.

Is this a pitch for a big project?

No. We propose one small, defined first step, and the next move is yours. No pressure, no manufactured urgency.

What does it cost?

The Blueprint is complimentary: a free session and a written summary, then an optional deeper dive if it fits. If you decide to go further, we scope a Proof Project separately, and no step obligates the next.
